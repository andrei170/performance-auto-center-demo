The Story

Two partners. One family-owned shop.

Performance Auto Center has operated under the same brand for 50 years in Elk Grove Village, Illinois. Family-owned. Owner-operated. Two partners between two locations. Craig Behrens runs the Elk Grove Village flagship at 1051 Bonaventure Drive. Pat Kirchens runs the Plainfield location.

The credential stack is what makes the shop unusual in the independent European category. ASE Master Certified is the tier the American auto-service industry uses to mark its most thoroughly tested technicians. Both partners hold it. Pat for 20-plus years, Craig for 12. Manufacturer advanced certification is the second tier, granted by the marque itself for advanced training on specific platforms. Pat holds it in BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Porsche at the same time. Craig holds it in Acura, Honda, Lexus, and Toyota at the same time. Seven manufacturer advanced certifications, two ASE Master Certifications, 50-plus combined years on the floor.
