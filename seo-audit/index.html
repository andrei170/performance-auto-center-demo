Finding 01 · Structural

The LinkNow Media template is the ceiling.

Your site runs on a LinkNow Media platform (MX records confirm: mail3.linknowmedia.cloud and mail4.linknowmedia.cloud). LinkNow templates ship with locked title-tag patterns, no per-page schema control, and limited sitemap access. Until the platform changes, every SEO recommendation downstream is capped by what LinkNow will let you publish.

Finding 02 · Directories

You are absent from BimmerShops, BenzShops, and Pcarwise.

These are the three directories AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) scrape when a user asks for an independent European-marque shop in a metro. Your direct local competitors Mehta Motors and 7th Gear appear in BimmerShops. You do not. This is the root cause of the 0-of-5 AEO result on luxury-European queries near Elk Grove Village.

Finding 03 · Schema

Your 5 of 5 / 283 CARFAX reviews are invisible to engines.

No AggregateRating schema is published anywhere on the site. The single strongest reputational signal in your entire pipeline currently exists only as text inside a CARFAX widget. Search engines and AI scrapers cannot read it. Adding the schema is a one-day technical job that surfaces the strongest moat you have.

Section 02 · Technical and On-Page Findings

What is actually on the page versus what should be.

Each finding below is observable from the public site as of audit date. Eight items, ordered by remediation priority.

LinkNow Media template caps schema and title-tag control

MX lookup confirms LinkNow infrastructure. Their templates do not expose per-page JSON-LD insertion, custom sitemap rules, or canonical title-tag overrides at the level needed for AEO and luxury-marque ranking.

Why it matters: every fix below either gets shipped properly on a real platform or gets shipped as a workaround that engines deprioritize. Fix: Phase 2 platform-migration assessment to WordPress or Webflow. Preserve URLs, redirect map, content. Estimated 14 day project.

Not listed on BimmerShops, BenzShops, or Pcarwise

The three directories AI engines pull from for European-marque independent shop recommendations. Submission is free or low-cost and takes under a week to propagate.

Why it matters: this single action is the highest-impact week in the entire 60 day plan. It closes the AEO gap that is currently sending European-marque searches to Mehta Motors and 7th Gear. Fix: Day 1 submission to BimmerShops Elite tier, BenzShops, Pcarwise. Apply for any verification badges available.

No AggregateRating schema for CARFAX 5 of 5 / 283 reviews

The CARFAX score is the strongest verifiable third-party reputational signal in the file. It does not appear in JSON-LD anywhere on the site, so it cannot show up as a star rating in Google results or get cited by AI engines.

Why it matters: this is the lowest-effort, highest-credibility win on the page. One schema block, immediately citable by every AI engine that reads structured data. Fix: AggregateRating JSON-LD with ratingValue 5, reviewCount 283, sourced URL pointing to the CARFAX listing. Ship Day 1.

No AutomotiveBusiness, Person, or Service schema

Pat Kirchens holds ASE Master plus advanced certifications on BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Porsche across 30 years in the industry. Craig Behrens holds ASE Master plus Acura, Honda, Lexus, Toyota advanced. None of it is structured for machines to read.

Why it matters: engines cite people, not pages. Without Person schema and EducationalOccupationalCredential nodes per certification, the partners are invisible to entity-based ranking. Fix: Deploy AutomotiveBusiness plus AutoRepair plus 2x Person (Craig, Pat) plus EducationalOccupationalCredential nodes for each ASE Master and marque-specific certification. Phase 1.

EGV and Plainfield need separate LocalBusiness schemas

Two locations currently share most public-facing infrastructure. Each needs its own LocalBusiness JSON-LD with distinct NAP (Name, Address, Phone), distinct GBP linkage, and distinct service-area markup.

Why it matters: Google treats multi-location shops as separate entities. Sharing schema or NAP across both halves the ranking power of each. Fix: Two LocalBusiness blocks, deployed alongside each location landing page. Phase 1.

Homepage title tag is generic

The current title does not mention BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Lexus, the 50 year history, or Elk Grove Village. It reads as a placeholder. Title tag is still one of the strongest on-page ranking signals for local intent.

Why it matters: the title tag is the single most-edited field in any SEO sprint. Fixing it costs ten minutes and lifts CTR on every existing impression. Fix: Rewrite to "BMW Mercedes Porsche Lexus Specialist EGV IL · 50 Years Family-Owned · Performance Auto Center" or a tested variant. Phase 1.

Plainfield exact street address not surfaced Kickoff Flag

The Plainfield location appears in references but the exact street address could not be verified from the public site during this audit.

Why it matters: NAP consistency is the foundation of local SEO. Address mismatches across GBP, schema, and the site itself collapse local ranking authority. Fix: Confirm Plainfield street address at kickoff. Apply identically across schema, GBP, footer, and contact page.

GBP optimization needed for both locations

Both Google Business Profile listings need: claim verification, service categories aligned to BMW / Mercedes-Benz / Porsche / Lexus specialist intent, post cadence reactivated, photo refresh, and Q and A seeded with the questions luxury-marque owners actually ask.

Why it matters: GBP is where 70 percent of local intent currently converts before users ever reach the site. Fix: Audit, claim, and optimize both GBP listings. Phase 1.

Section 04 · 60-Day Action Plan

Three phases. Sequenced by impact.

Each phase compounds the previous one. Phase 1 alone closes most of the AEO and credibility gap. Phases 2 and 3 widen the moat.

Phase 01 Foundation and Directory Capture Days 1 to 14

Phase 02 Platform and Per-Marque Depth Days 15 to 30
  • Days 15 to 18: Platform migration assessment. Scope the LinkNow to WordPress or Webflow move. Build URL preservation map, identify content that needs porting, set redirect plan.
  • Days 18 to 25: Build per-marque landing pages: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Lexus. Each page targets its marque-specific service queries (oil service intervals, common repair intent, certification visibility).
  • Days 25 to 30: Build Craig Behrens and Pat Kirchens credential pages. Each includes full ASE Master detail, marque-specific advanced certs, years in industry, and a verifiable third-party reference where one exists. These are the pages AI engines cite when asked "who works on Porsche in EGV".

  • Days 31 to 38: Build the 50-year heritage authority page. Founded 1976. Family ownership lineage. Verifiable via Yellowpages cross-reference. This is the page that earns links and gets cited in any "oldest independent shop" query.

  • Days 50 to 60: CARFAX integration extension. Surface individual review excerpts on-page in a way that creates additional indexable Review schema, beyond the aggregate. Set the baseline Google Search Console performance report for ongoing measurement.
